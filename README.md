# İz Haritası — Google Timeline Location History Heatmap

A free, browser-based tool that turns your Google Maps Timeline data into a beautiful location heatmap in seconds.

**🔗 Live tool:** https://aliozgurr.github.io/timeline-heatmap/

## What does it do?

It builds a heatmap showing where you've traveled over the years and which cities or regions you've spent the most time in, at a glance. Supports the current Google Timeline export as well as older Google Takeout and Semantic Location History formats.

## How to use it

1. On your phone: Google Maps → Settings → **Location and privacy** → **Export Timeline data**
2. Drag and drop the downloaded JSON file onto https://aliozgurr.github.io/timeline-heatmap/
3. Explore your heatmap, filter by date range and intensity

## Privacy

Your location data is processed entirely in your own browser; it is never sent to, saved on, or stored on any server.
